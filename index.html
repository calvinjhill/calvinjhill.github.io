About Me

Hi, I'm Calvin. My tech career started as a Business Analyst for a large healthcare system. After a couple of years, I earned a promotion to Sr. Business Analyst. Then, a few years later, I earned a promotion to Manager of Analytics. A few years after that I became Director of Analytics. Along the way, I completed my Master's in Data Science & Analytics.

My real-world experience and formal training are what set me apart. Earning my degree has expanded my knowledge of data and analytics and work has taught me how to use it. Now I help Small Business Owners with their analytics and web development needs. In my spare time, I love traveling with my wife, reading, good food, technology and sports.
